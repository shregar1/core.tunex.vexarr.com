# Code of Conduct

TuneX contributors are expected to work respectfully and professionally.

## Standards

- Focus critique on technical behavior, design tradeoffs, and evidence.
- Assume good intent, but be precise about risks and regressions.
- Keep discussions accessible to contributors across experience levels.
- Do not harass, threaten, insult, or exclude contributors.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the project maintainers through the repository's
private security or maintainer contact channel.
